The Ultimate Recipe Breakdown

☕ Coffee: 30g (Medium Coarse) 💧 Water: 500g (Boiling)
  1. Add coffee to the French Press, pour 500g of boiling water, and let it steep.
  2. [0:00 - 4:00] Let it steep for 4 minutes undisturbed.
  3. [4:00 - 4:05] Break the crust! Give the top layer a gentle stir.
  4. [4:05 - 4:15] Scoop out the floating white foam and remaining floating bits.
  5. [4:15 - 9:05] Wait 5 minutes for the coffee to settle and cool. **Do not plunge!**
  6. [9:05] Put the lid on, place the filter right on top of the liquid, and pour gently without plunging down.
