The Ultimate Recipe Breakdown

☕ Coffee: 11g (Medium-Fine) 💧 Water: 200g (Boiling)
  1. Put a paper filter in the cap. Assemble and add coffee.
  2. Pour 200g of water as quickly as possible.
  3. Immediately insert the plunger (~1cm) to create a vacuum seal.
  4. [0:00 - 2:00] Let it steep for 2 minutes.
  5. [2:00 - 2:05] Give it a gentle swirl to drop the crust.
  6. [2:05 - 2:35] Wait 30 seconds for settling.
  7. [2:35 - 3:05] Press down very gently (takes ~30 seconds).
